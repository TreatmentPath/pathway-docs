How to read it
Arrows follow the direction data is written. Every arrow into the identity core (Person → PersonChannel → ContactChannel) passes the pre-save signal pipeline — except the two thick red dashed edges (Go writers), which bypass it.
P1 · Record-level contact columns — the duplication root. Patient/Intake/Nurture each hold their own email/phone copies, written by 17+ lanes with different strictness. Any matcher reading these columns inherits the drift. All raw/partial lanes (red/amber nodes) feed this.
P2 · Go writers bypass Django signals — Dentally sync (raw SQL) and Call Agent (GORM) write Patient/Intake/Person/Channel rows directly; set_patient_person, propagation and merge signals never fire. Person/channel resolution is re-implemented in Go.
P3 · Online Booking writes raw — payment-converted Patients and abandoned-session Intakes store unnormalised email/phone (services.py:507, tasks.py:118).
P4 · AI email intake & CSV import — FIXED — AI-extraction lane (2026-09-03); CSV import email now strip+lower, and a row that DEDUPS onto an existing patient now contributes its channels to that Person instead of discarding them (the dedup was manufacturing the next duplicate). 2026-09-04.
P6 · Dentally tables sit OUTSIDE the identity graph — RecallPatient (32k), RecallRecord (32k), DentallyAppointment (556k), DaylistPatient (9k) carry email/phone but have no person FK and no channel FK, and are written by Go. Their contacts can never participate in dedup. Email drift was ongoing, not legacy (Sep-2026: DentallyAppointment 251, DaylistPatient 6) — writers now canonicalise, but the structural gap (no channel linkage) remains open.
P7 · Call-agent weld window — measured 2026-09-04: zero call-agent intakes get a Person at insert. 1445/1447 are welded only when Django later re-saves the row — median under 24h, tail to 59 days. The 7 permanent orphans are correct (no usable channel: "anonymous" caller, no email) bar one with a valid number.
P5 · Oversized households — shared generic values have grouped huge "families" (a 202-member household exists); needs capping/review (Phase 5 optional step).
F1 · Family sibling adoption — FIXED — conversion matcher now prefers the record's own Person link and rejects cross-Person adoption (journey/mixins.py).
F2 · Ingest boundary normalisation — FIXED — normalize_lead_contact (strip+lower email, trim) at all four webhook/form/AI-email lanes; phones parsed at all lanes.
F3 · Conversion creation writes canonical values — FIXED — new Patients get lowercased email + E.164 phone; forced target_patient_id links the exact picked patient.
F4 · ONE normalizer per key, cross-language — FIXED — Django had ~20 inline email copies and three name normalizers with two behaviours: the duplicate FINDER collapsed internal whitespace, Person.resolve (the RESOLVER) did not, so the finder reported matches the resolver refused to act on. Now one definition (utils/contact_keys.py: canonical_email, canonical_name_part) with every identity-critical caller delegating, mirrored in Go by pkg/email + pkg/personname and pinned to Django by a shared fixture (contact_key_fixtures.json) — the same mechanism that already guards pkg/phone. 2026-09-04.
F5 · Identity truth-table pinned to the backend — FIXED — the 8 name/email/phone cases are now asserted against real ingests in test_identity_resolution_matrix.py. Cells 2 and 5 had never been exercised by any test. Case 5 (same name, all-new email and phone) can never merge — name is not a candidacy signal — so a returning patient with new contact details is a permanently invisible duplicate. Case 4 (all channels match, name differs) splits by design and is handed to the manual merge flow.
F6 · Ingress harness verdicts were unfalsifiable — FIXED — the scorer keyed each test record by email, so the 7 of 14 scenarios that share an email between records always scored correct_merge regardless of what the product did. Refs are now keyed to the DB row their own delivery landed on. 2026-09-04.
